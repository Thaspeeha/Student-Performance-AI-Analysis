## ⚠️ Where AI Was Cautious (100% Honest)

### 🔸 Causation vs Correlation

- I described relationships but didn't claim causation.
- The data shows students who study more score higher,
  but I can't prove studying causes higher scores.
- High performers may simply choose to study more.

---

### 🔸 "Multiplicative" Language

- I used this term to describe how the factors work together.
- The combined effects are clearly visible in the data.
- However, I did **not** run statistical interaction tests.

---

### 🔸 "Irrelevant" for Study Method

- I described the **1.4-point difference** as *nearly irrelevant*.
- This is a practical judgment rather than a statistical conclusion.
- The difference exists but is tiny compared with the **45-point study-hours gap**.

---

## 🚫 What the AI Couldn't Verify

### 📌 Statistical Significance

- No p-values were calculated.
- No confidence intervals were generated.
- Therefore, small differences cannot be confirmed as statistically significant.

---

### 📌 Confounding Variables

The analysis could not verify factors such as:

- 🎯 Motivation
- 📖 Prior knowledge
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family support
- 🧠 Other unmeasured influences
